# Voice Lint

Copy this file next to your other rules, or into `.claude/rules/`.

**A pass/fail gate for anything you will read or that leaves your machine.** Posts, emails, DMs,
reports, documents. Every item is binary. Fix violations before the draft reaches you.

This file starts mostly empty on purpose. **It fills up from your corrections log**, as rules hit
three strikes and get promoted here. A rule in this file should always be traceable to a real edit
you actually made. That is what keeps it from becoming a wishlist nobody applies.

---

## Always

Shipped defaults. These are safe for anyone.

- [ ] Nothing invented: no fabricated facts, quotes, numbers, names, or details. Gaps become open
      questions, not filler.
- [ ] No em dashes or en dashes. Default substitute is a colon, otherwise restructure. Hyphens stay
      in number ranges (4-8) and compound modifiers (well-known).
- [ ] One idea per piece. A second consequence or example is a second idea.
- [ ] No AI structure patterns: staccato triplets, "It's not X. It's Y.", "Most people / the best
      ones," "Read that again," rhetorical "Why? Because...", a closer that restates the hook.
- [ ] No banned openers: "I've been thinking about...", "Let me share...", "Here's the thing..."
- [ ] Names, quotes, and client details that need someone's permission are flagged, not quietly
      anonymized and not silently used.

## Yours

Promoted from your corrections log. Empty at install.

- [ ]

## External-facing

Anything going to a prospect, a client, or an audience.

- [ ] Any price or commitment quoted matches what you actually offer today.
- [ ] Links are the ones you meant, and they work.
- [ ] Leads with something useful. No bio recitation.
- [ ] Nothing that reads as generated: no template scaffolding, no hollow personalization.

## Messages you send

Email, DM, chat.

- [ ] Full sentences. Do not clip the leading subject: "Still want to get you in front of the group"
      becomes "I still want to get you in front of the group." Posts keep deliberate fragments.
      Person-to-person messages do not.
- [ ] Plain and warm. Soft, low-pressure asks.

## Posts

- [ ] The hook earns the click, and the first line is not a soft opener.
- [ ] The closer is a line, not a question. The exception is a genuine ask post.
- [ ] The CTA is qualified or absent. No "comment below," no "comment YES."
- [ ] The ghostwriter test passes: at least one sentence only you could have written.
